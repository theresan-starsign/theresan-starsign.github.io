About Me

Hi! I'm Theresa Nyowheoma (that's "yo-way-oh-mah")

I'm a first year grad student at the Pratt Institute, working towards an MS in Library and Information Science. I plan on becoming a public librarian after graduation, but am keeping my options open to other paths such as museum education and academic or research librarianship.

Since undergrad, I've gained a lot of experience in educational environments, through substitute teaching and helping run competitive quiz bowl tournaments. I see libraries as an extension of a school environment, but with so much more room to explore interests, continually learn, and feed curiosity.
